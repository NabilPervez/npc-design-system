One-line: signature-talk card for the speaker profile's keynote grid.

```jsx
<TalkCard title="The Agile Creative" category="Strategy"
  summary="Creatives hate “process,” but they need it to scale."
  bullets={['Run a “Creative Sprint” without killing inspiration.','The “Definition of Done” for ambiguous assets.']} />
```

Bullets use the em-dash marker, never discs or checkmarks.
